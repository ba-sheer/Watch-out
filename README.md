# Watch out

A top-down dodger about being very small in a very large house.

## How to play

| | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | `W A S D` or arrow keys | left stick |
| Dash | `Space` | `A` |
| Pause | `Esc` or `P` | `Start` |
| Pick a world | `A` `D` or `←` `→` | d-pad |
| Mute | `M` | |
| Fullscreen | `F11` | |

**The rules, in short**
- 3 hearts: Each hit costs 1 heart and grants 1.5s of grace.
- Dash: Lasts 0.15s, prevents damage, and has a 1s cooldown.
- Close calls: Dodge hazards closely to boost combo and score.
- Combos: Chain pickups and close calls within 3s.
- Pickups: Common (+10 pts), Golden (+50 pts, double combo), Healing (+1 heart). They attract you nearby and vanish after 9s.
- Best scores: Saved locally for each world.

| World | Falls from above | Rolls across | Chases you | Snacks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Kitchen** | Slipper | Rolling pin | Roomba | Crumb · Cheese · Golden Crumb |
| **Bathroom** | Sandal | Toilet paper | Roomba | Bubble · Soap · Rubber Duck |
| **Backyard** | Boot | Log | Lawn mower | Berry · Apple · Golden Acorn |
| **Garage** | Boot | Pipe | Henry | Bolt · Battery · Golden Gear |

Every world is rated **EASY**, **MEDIUM**, **HARD** or **EXTREME** on the title screen. The rating is worked out from that world's own numbers, so it can't fall out of date when you tune things.
